## Source Decisions And Uncertainty

- [VCA: Conures](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/conures-general): group-level cage guidance, not a guarantee for each Sun Conure. Different primary care sheets give different minima; do not silently combine them into an approved universal standard.
- [Tree of Life: Conure Care](https://treeoflifeexotics.vet/education-resource-center/for-clients/birds/conure-care): attributed bar-gap guidance only. Does not validate an illustrated cage or a purchased product.
- [VCA: Feeding Pet Conures](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/conures-feeding): source-specific questions. Percentage recommendations on the source page are not combined into a Birbest formula; no fixed food-withdrawal schedule adopted.
- [AAV: Providing Adequate Sleep](https://www.aav.org/blogpost/1778905/376627/Providing-Adequate-Sleep): indexed article text retrieved; direct page returned 403. Individual advice, not inherited Cockatiel sleep hours.
- [Merck: Providing a Home](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/bird-owners/choosing-and-taking-care-of-a-pet-bird/providing-a-home-for-a-bird): environment and usable space.
- [VCA: Introducing a New Pet Bird](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/introducing-a-new-pet-bird): examination and separate-room introduction context, not guaranteed compatibility.
- [VCA: Cage Hygiene](https://vcahospitals.com/vitality/know-your-pet/cage-hygiene-in-birds): hygiene principles; no new disinfectant recipe or HOCl claim.
- [WPT: Positive Reinforcement](https://parrots.org/focus/improving-parrot-care/the-power-of-positive-reinforcement/): choice and reward-based interaction.
- [University of Florida: Pet Bird Care](https://smallanimal.vethospital.ufl.edu/clinical-services/zoological-medicine/how-to-care-for-your-pet-bird/): relevant housing/enrichment principles only; no forced handling or food restriction adopted.
- [Merck: Household Hazards](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/bird-owners/routine-care-and-safety-of-birds/household-hazards-for-pet-birds), [VCA: Signs of Illness](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/recognizing-the-signs-of-illness-in-pet-birds), [Merck: Injuries](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/bird-owners/disorders-and-diseases-of-birds/injuries-and-accidents-of-pet-birds): escalation references, not diagnosis or home treatment.
